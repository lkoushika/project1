Meet Mittens

This is a website about an animal, the cat!! Mittens is a fluffy gray cat with white paws. She is very lazy but also very cute. She likes to sit and stare until she gets her food.

Name: Mittens
Favorite food: tuna
Favorite place: the warm spot by the window
Favorite hobby: knocking things off the table

Cool Cat Facts

  • Cats sleep about 12 to 16 hours every day
  • A group of kittens is called a litter
  • Cats purr when they are happy (and sometimes when they are hurt)
  • Cats can jump about 5 times their own height
  • Every cat's nose print is different, like a fingerprint
